# Supplier Terms (Tempo)

Future supplier credit tracking should include supplier, invoice, received date, due date, amount, outstanding balance, partial payments, payment history, and invoice attachment. Support unpaid, due soon, overdue, and paid states derived consistently from balances and dates. Plan WhatsApp reminders through a future server-side integration with consent, delivery tracking, and secret credentials kept off the client. No supplier debt module is implemented yet.

Receipt acquisition-cost evidence is separate from supplier payable. In C3A, OWNER/ADMIN may record a protected provisional estimate per receipt item; it is not a finalized supplier invoice and does not wait for payment. Bonus quantity joins usable stock quantity; rejected/short quantities are excluded. Invoice-to-receipt allocation and later provisional-to-final cost adjustments are not implemented in C3A.
